# 🏥 Hospital Emergency Room Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

[![Live Dashboard](https://img.shields.io/badge/PowerBI-View_Dashboard-yellow?style=for-the-badge&logo=powerbi)](https://app.powerbi.com/view?r=eyJrIjoiNTg5NzhkMTEtNTk5NC00ZDEwLTkzNDUtMDgwOWVkMDg4Y2ViIiwidCI6ImRmODY3OWNkLWE4MGUtNDVkOC05OWFjLWM4M2VkN2ZmOTVhMCJ9)

This interactive **Power BI dashboard** analyzes Emergency Room performance across key metrics like patient flow, wait time, satisfaction, referrals & demographics to help hospital management take **data-driven operational decisions**.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Pages & Purpose

| Page | Focus |
|------|------|
| **Monthly View** | Trend analysis of KPIs month-wise |
| **Consolidated View** | Overall performance summary for any date period |
| **Patient Details** | Granular patient-level view with slicers |
| **Key Takeaways** | Business recommendations for improvement |

✨ All pages include **navigation buttons** for enhanced UX.

---

## ⭐ Key KPIs Measured

- Total Patients Visited  
- Average Wait Time (mins)  
- Patient Satisfaction Score  
- Number of Patients Referred  
- Admission Rate  
- % Seen Within 30 Minutes  
- Age Group & Race Distribution  
- Day/Hour Volume Analysis (Heatmap)

---

## 🧠 Insights & Recommendations

| Insight | Impact | Recommendation |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| Peak hours: **10 AM – 2 PM** & weekends | Longer queues | Allocate additional triage staff |
| **Avg wait time = 35.3 mins** | Satisfaction gets impacted | Fast-track queue for minor cases |
| **52% admitted patients** | Bed capacity strain | Optimize bed allocation |
| Highest visits: **Age 20–39** | Injury/Workload cases | Awareness & preventive measures |
| High referrals: GP & Orthopedics | Specialist dependency | More specialist coverage |

> Improving triage speed during peak hours can significantly enhance service quality.
