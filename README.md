# 🏥 Hospital Emergency Room Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

[![Live Dashboard](https://img.shields.io/badge/PowerBI-View_Dashboard-yellow?style=for-the-badge&logo=powerbi)](https://app.powerbi.com/view?r=eyJrIjoiNTg5NzhkMTEtNTk5NC00ZDEwLTkzNDUtMDgwOWVkMDg4Y2ViIiwidCI6ImRmODY3OWNkLWE4MGUtNDVkOC05OWFjLWM4M2VkN2ZmOTVhMCJ9)

This interactive **Power BI dashboard** provides insights into Emergency Room performance, patient experience, and operational efficiency.  
It helps hospital management **optimize resources & improve patient care** through data-driven decisions.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Pages & Purpose

| Page | Objective |
|------|----------|
| **Monthly View** | Analyze monthly patient trends & KPIs |
| **Consolidated View** | Full-period analytics with date range filter |
| **Patient Details** | Patient-level drilldown for case-level analysis |
| **Key Takeaways** | Summary of actionable insights for management |

---

## 🚑 Business Challenges Addressed

- Long patient wait times impacting satisfaction  
- Workforce shortages during **peak hours**  
- Poor referral coordination and bed management  
- Increased ER visits from **young working population**  
- Operational overload on **Mondays & weekends**

---

## 🔢 Key Metrics (KPIs) Visualized

- Total Patients  
- Avg. Wait Time  
- Satisfaction Score  
- No. of Patients Referred  
- Admission Ratio  
- % Seen Within Target Time  
- Age & Race Distribution  
- Hourly & Weekday Volume Analysis

## 🧠 Data Insights & Actions

| Insight | Impact | Recommendation |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| Peak rush: 10AM–2PM & weekends | Overload | Increase triage staff |
| Avg Wait Time: 35.3 min | Low satisfaction | Fast-track queue |
| 52% admitted cases | Bed shortage | Improve bed allocation |
| Age 20–39 highest visits | Injury cases high | Preventive awareness |
| Highest referrals: GP & Ortho | Specialist limitation | More specialist shifts |
